Featured Rafting Packages

Salmon River Half-Day

Difficulty: Class I - II (Beginner)

Perfect for families and first-timers! Experience gentle floats, beautiful scenic canyon views, and fun swimming holes.

Grand Canyon Full-Day

Difficulty: Class III - IV (Intermediate)

Our most popular trip! Tackle splashy whitewater during the day and enjoy a riverside lunch midway through the run.

Hell's Canyon Expedition

Difficulty: Class IV - V (Advanced)

A multi-day adrenaline thrill ride for experienced rafters. Navigate massive drops, fast currents, and steep canyon walls.
